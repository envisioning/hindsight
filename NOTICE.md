## Third-party material

Claims quote the publications they come from. Hindsight keeps only facts (who said what, when, about which subject, with which timing) and short attributed quotes. It does not reproduce charts, figures or full text from any publication. Each claim records its source and edition, so any claim can be traced back to the publisher.
