## Exception: Banco Central do Brasil Focus data

`data/raw/bcb-focus/` is derived from the Banco Central do Brasil open data portal (the Focus market expectations survey via the Olinda API, and SGS series for realized values). The BCB publishes that data under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0), https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/.

The files in `data/raw/bcb-focus/`, and any database derived from them, are published under ODbL 1.0, not CC BY 4.0. Attribute them as "Banco Central do Brasil, Focus market expectations survey and SGS, via Envisioning Hindsight". The rest of the dataset stays under CC BY 4.0.
