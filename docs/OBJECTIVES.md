# Research objectives

What Hindsight is for, and how far each objective has come. Agents read this with `docs/HANDOFF.md` and update it when an objective moves: change the state line, add the issue, and record any change of objective as a decision in `docs/DECISIONS.md` approved by MZ. Do not change an objective without MZ.

Last reviewed: 2026-10-03.

## 1. A citable public record of published forecasts

Every published forecast we can find, as a dated claim with a source and a stable id, graded against what happened, with the evidence and the error rate beside every figure.

- **State:** 37 sources captured as far as public copies exist (about 28,300 claims). Every source graded under D20 with audits (D11, D28). Releases 2026.1 and 2026.2 published.
- **Next:** #76 release 2026.3 (with links); #92 DOI and Dataset markup; #94 the yearly January cycle; #58 monthly refresh; gaps with no public copy: #26, #27.

## 2. Anticipation: how well institutions see ahead

Accuracy per publication, side by side and never ranked (D6, D17), and how accuracy changes with the horizon.

- **State:** hit rates and intervals for every judgment and numeric source; trend persistence (D33); ranking and scenario measures (D31 to D34).
- **Next:** #87 accuracy by horizon; #88 herding and joint misses.

## 3. Adaptability: whether institutions update

The positioning question: do institutions revise their expectations when the evidence changes, and how fast? Adaptation lag is the metric named in the 2026-09-27 pilot spec.

- **State:** not measured yet. Revisions (682), numeric vintages and ranking histories are already in the data.
- **Next:** #86 adaptation lag; #90 tie-in to the capability benchmarks (needs MZ's call on a data link); #89 the adaptability audit as a client product.

## 4. Imagination: what fiction saw first (Origins)

Imagination lead time: years from a work's depiction of a technology to its first prototype, first product and mainstream adoption, over a defined canon of works. Fiction is never graded right or wrong (D6).

- **State:** 76 hand-made works in www; plan decided by MZ 2026-10-02.
- **Next:** epic #85 (#77 to #84).

## 5. One connected map of expectations and technologies

Forecasts, fiction and Envisioning's research database joined through shared subjects, so a reader moves between what was expected, what was imagined and what exists.

- **State:** 1,529 verified subject-technology links (D48 to D56), shown on research, claim and subject pages.
- **Next:** #74 links upkeep; #83 Origins links.

The record answers the questions people search for and is easy to cite correctly.

- **Next:** #91 finding pages (starting with the Hype Cycle report the pilot spec promised); #93 structured data and citation blocks; #92 DOI; #94 annual report.
