# Envisioning the future of health technology (2012)

Envisioning Technology's radial poster of health technologies, footer "Updated: September 2012", published at envisioningtech.com/health/ under CC BY-SA. Source: Wayback Machine snapshots of the page (2014-07-07), the full-resolution PNG (2200x2600, first archived 2012-09-12) and the screen PDF.

Files: `2012.json` (placements), `PROGRESS.md`.

## How time was read

The wheel has six sectors (Regeneration, Treatments, Biogerontology, Telemedicine, Diagnostics, Augmentation) and three concentric bands around a central hub, labelled at their outer edge 2020, 2030 and 2040. The hub is the poster's present (2012); there is no start label. A dot is read as the year of the ring that closes its band (2020, 2030 or 2040). A dot centred on a ring is read as that ring's year, low confidence. Band widths are not proportional to years, so position inside a band is noted but not converted to a year.

Ring radii measured on the PNG: hub edge about 190px, 2020 ring 507px, 2030 ring 721px, 2040 outer edge 891px, centre (1097, 1400). Dot centres were found by pixel analysis; labels came from the PDF text layer and were checked on full-resolution tiles.

## Counts

- 55 placements (all `technology`): 28 at 2020, 21 at 2030, 6 at 2040.
- Confidence: 45 high, 5 medium, 5 low (dots on a ring).
- Not captured: 11 cluster icons (tree roots such as 3D printers, mHealth, Big data, Cryonics) that group the dots. They are not time placements.

## Caveats for graders

The poster does not say "mainstream". It speculates on technologies "likely to affect" or "disrupt" health care in the coming decades. Several labels are speculative end-states (full brain simulation, reverse cryonics, suspended animation). Claims read "<Label> starts to affect health care around <year>" (D37). See `notes` in `2012.json`.
