# Envisioning education poster (2012)

"Envisioning the future of education technology", a one-page A3 poster published by Envisioning (then envisioningtech.com) in June 2012. The PDF footer credits envisioningtech.com, tferesearch.com and Marie Curie FP7; the 2012 web page describes it as a collaboration with TFE Research. About 42 technologies are laid out on a vertical timeline 2012-2040 under three loose columns (Classroom, Studio, Virtual) and linked to six hub concepts.

Files: `2012.json` (placements, captured, not graded), `PROGRESS.md`.

Sources: https://www.envisioning.com/work/education (image), web.archive.org snapshot of envisioningtech.com/education/ (2012-06-28) and of the PDF (PDF creation date 2012-06-26).

## How time was read

- Year labels 2012, 2020, 2030, 2040 sit on the left; between them the background alternates light/dark bands, one band per year (7 bands between 2012 and 2020, 9 between 2020 and 2030, 9 between 2030 and 2040). The scale compresses: about 80px per year before 2020, 62px in the 2020s, 52px in the 2030s on the 2480px image.
- The top band holding the 2012 label (y 421-657) is taller than the rest; every node in it is read as 2012.
- Each leaf circle is read at the band containing its centre (centres from the PDF text layer, checked against full-resolution tiles). Within 15px of a band edge: medium. Within 4px: read as the later year, low.
- Hubs (Digitized classrooms, Gamification, Opening of information, Disintermediation, Tangible computing, Virtual/physical studios) are concepts, not placements. They are recorded only as each leaf's `category`, from circle colour.

## What a placement means

The poster does not say "mainstream". It organises technologies "likely to influence education in the upcoming decades" and says the scenarios will "start panning out in learning environments". Placements are about use in education, not general adoption. Claims read "<Label> starts to influence learning environments around <year>" (D37); graders test use in schools and learning, not general adoption.
