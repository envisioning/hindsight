# Banco Central do Brasil Focus survey: annual market expectations

Publisher: Banco Central do Brasil (BCB). Series: Focus market expectations survey (Sistema Expectativas de Mercado). The weekly "Focus - Relatório de Mercado" shows these medians every Monday, with data up to the Friday before.
Facts only: indicator, target year, median expectation, survey date, source. Nothing here is graded and no errors are computed.
This file is generated by `scripts/bcb-focus/build.py` from `scripts/bcb-focus/index_header.md`. Edit the header there.

## Scope

- Indicators (Focus label in the API, `Indicador`):
  - `IPCA`: IPCA inflation, December to December, %. Subject `brazil-ipca-inflation`.
  - `PIB Total`: real GDP growth, %. Subject `brazil-real-gdp-growth`.
  - `Selic`: Copom Selic target rate at year end, % p.a. (the effective Over-Selic rate at year end for surveys before 2004-04-16, see below). Subject `brazil-selic-rate`.
  - `Câmbio`: USD/BRL exchange rate at year end, BRL per USD. Subject `brazil-usd-brl-exchange-rate`.
- Statistic: the median (`Mediana`) of all forecasts submitted in the 30 days up to the survey date (`baseCalculo` 0). This is the statistic the Focus report shows. The API also has `baseCalculo` 1 (forecasts from the last 5 business days only); it is not used.
- Horizons: the current calendar year (`horizon: current_year`) and the next year (`horizon: next_year`). 8 entries per full edition.
- Sample: one survey per quarter, the last survey date in March, June, September and December. Edition id `YYYY-MM`. `published` and each entry's `survey_date` are the exact `Data` of that survey in the API.
- Editions: 1999-06 to 2026-06 (109 quarter slots). 1999-06 has no in-scope indicator. 2026-09 is not yet available.

## Verify-first answers (issue #18)

- **Start date.** The BCB started the market expectations survey in May 1999 (BCB FAQ "Sistema Expectativas de Mercado", series Perguntas Mais Frequentes no. 13, information up to June 2016, https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/controleinflacao/Documents/Expectativas_mercado/FAQ13-Expectativas-de-Mercado.pdf). The web page of the system started in November 2001. In the Olinda API, `PIB Total` starts 1999-07-01; `IPCA`, `Selic` and `Câmbio` start 2000-01-03. The IGP indices start 1999-04-30 but are out of scope.
- **Data access.** Olinda OData API, entity `ExpectativasMercadoAnuais`: https://olinda.bcb.gov.br/olinda/servico/Expectativas/versao/v1/odata/ExpectativasMercadoAnuais . Filter by `Indicador` and `Data`, `$format=json`. The series is daily (business days), not weekly. Each entry's `source_url` is the OData query that returns that indicator on that survey date. No authentication.
- **Terms of reuse.** The BCB open data portal lists the dataset "Expectativas de Mercado" (https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/dataset/expectativas-mercado) under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL), `license_id: odc-odbl` in the portal's CKAN API (package metadata modified 2026-09-11). The SGS series used for realized values are also listed under ODbL on the same portal (confirmed for SGS 432, https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/dataset/432-taxa-de-juros---meta-selic-definida-pelo-copom; the portal FAQ states that BCB open data use ODbL). ODbL requires attribution and keeps derived databases that are made public under ODbL (share-alike). See open problems.
- **Evaluations (comparison, not used for grading).** The BCB Inflation Report publishes boxes on Focus forecast accuracy from time to time. Not collected here.

## Definitions and changes in the source

- **Exchange rate definition changed in 2021.** The BCB announced on 2021-01-15 that the Focus year-end exchange rate is the average PTAX sell rate of December, no longer the rate of the last business day of the year. In force from 2021-01-25 (Agência Brasil, https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/economia/noticia/2021-01/banco-central-anuncia-mudancas-no-boletim-focus). Each `Câmbio` entry has a `definition` field: last business day for survey dates before 2021-01-25, December average from that date. The same announcement extended the horizons (IPCA to 25 months, Selic, exchange rate and GDP to two years) and removed IGP-DI, IPA-DI, IPA-M, INPC, IPCA-15 and IPC-Fipe.
- **Selic before April 2004: Over-Selic, not the target (#66).** The weekly Focus report labels the Selic indicator "Taxa Over-Selic" / "Over-Selic - fim de período" (the effective overnight rate, SGS 1178) up to the report of 2004-04-08 and "Meta Taxa Selic - fim de período" (the Copom target, SGS 432) from the report of 2004-04-16. Checked in the reports of 2002-06-28, 2003-12-26 and every weekly report from 2004-01-02 to 2004-06-25, plus 2004-12-30 (https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/focus/focus/RYYYYMMDD.pdf). The API keeps one `Selic` series with no label change. The API data agree: the current-year Selic is collected up to the last business day of December in 2000 to 2003 (an effective year-end rate is unknown until then) and stops right after the December Copom meeting from 2004 (2004-12-15, 2005-12-14, ...). So the 2000-03 to 2004-03 editions forecast the Over-Selic at year end; from 2004-06 the target. `realized.json` holds SGS 1178 on the last business day of each year from 1999 to 2008 for them.
- **Selic and exchange rate before 2021.** Until 2021 the Focus collected both "end of period" and "period average" values for the Selic target and the exchange rate. The API holds one series per indicator. A spot check of the Focus report for 2019-06-28 (https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/focus/focus/R20190628.pdf, font-encoded, digits read by pattern) matched the API values with the end-of-period rows (Selic 5.50 for 2019 and 6.00 for 2020, exchange rate 3.80 for both years). Confidence stays `high` for the values; the mapping to "end of period" before 2021 rests on this one check.
- **Current year in December.** The Focus stops collecting the current-year Selic after the last Copom meeting of the year (the value is then known), and the current-year exchange rate on the last business days of December. December editions therefore have no current-year `Selic` and often no current-year `Câmbio`. The gap text in each file gives the last date with a value. These editions are `partial` by the file rule; the gaps are by design of the survey, not data loss. Values from earlier dates are not substituted.
- **Annual values change without new forecasts.** When IBGE publishes a monthly IPCA or a quarterly GDP figure, the system replaces the participant's monthly or quarterly forecast with the actual value and recomputes the annual figure the same day (BCB FAQ no. 13, question 13).
- **Survey date vs. weekly report.** The sample uses the last business day of the month in the daily API series. That day is often not a Friday, so the value can differ slightly from the weekly Focus report printed that month.
- **Respondents.** `numeroRespondentes` is null in the API for the sampled editions before 2014-03; `respondents` is then omitted.
- **1999 editions.** 1999-09 and 1999-12 have `PIB Total` only (IPCA, Selic and exchange rate series start 2000-01-03).

## realized.json

Realized values for 1999 to 2025 from the BCB SGS API (https://api.bcb.gov.br/dados/serie/bcdata.sgs.{code}/dados?formato=json), retrieved on the date in `vintage`. 2026 is excluded.

| SGS code | Series | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 13522 | IPCA accumulated over 12 months, monthly (IBGE) | December value = annual IPCA |
| 7326 | GDP real growth rate, annual (IBGE) | latest IBGE vintage, subject to revision |
| 432 | Selic target set by Copom, daily | value in force on the last date of the year |
| 1178 | Selic effective rate (Over-Selic), annualised, daily | value on the last business day of the year, 1999 to 2008; the Focus definition for surveys before 2004-04-16 |
| 3696 | USD/BRL PTAX sell, end of period, monthly | December = year-end rate, definition before 2021-01-25 |
| 3697 | USD/BRL PTAX sell, period average, monthly | December = year-end rate, definition from 2021-01-25 |

IPCA and GDP originate at IBGE; SGS republishes them. GDP values are the latest vintage, not first releases. No errors or grades are computed.

## Open problems

- **Licence fit.** ODbL share-alike applies to a derived database made public. Hindsight publishes `data/out` under CC BY 4.0 (NOTICE.md). Whether the Focus medians in Hindsight count as a "substantial" extract under ODbL, and whether those rows need ODbL terms, is a decision for MZ before release.
- **Pre-2021 Selic and exchange-rate definition** rests on one spot check (see above). The BCB methodology note for the 2021 change was not found on bcb.gov.br; the source is a news agency report.
- 2026-09 edition: the month has not ended in the API data (latest survey date 2026-09-18 on retrieval).
