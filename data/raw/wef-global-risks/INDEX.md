# WEF Global Risks Report: collected editions

Facts only: ranking, rank, label as printed, category, source. No charts or report text beyond labels (see NOTICE.md). Nothing here is graded.

## Verified first

- **First edition: 2006.** "Global Risks 2006", World Economic Forum with MMC, Merrill Lynch and Swiss Re, and the Wharton Risk Center (https://www3.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Global_Risks_Report_2006.pdf). The GRR 2011 calls itself the sixth edition, the GRR 2020 the 15th, the GRR 2021 the 16th. This is consistent with 2006 as edition 1. No earlier edition found.
- **2006 has no ranking.** It scores base-case and worst-case scenarios for likelihood (1-4, probability bands) and severity (1-4, US$ damage, deaths or GDP impact) to end-2006 and to 2015. The file records the scenarios on its two "highest severity" charts, without rank.
- **Is the ranking comparable across years? No, only within one method.** WEF's own retrospective figures say risks "may not be strictly comparable across years". Method blocks:

| Years | Ranking(s) | Method | Horizon |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2006 | none (scenario scores) | expert scenario scoring, likelihood 1-4, severity 1-4 | to end-2006; to 2015 |
| 2007-2010 | likelihood, impact (top 5) | Global Risk Network expert assessment; likelihood as probability, severity mainly US$ damage; 2009-2010 add a perception survey of about 120 experts | 10 years |
| 2011 | likelihood, impact (top 5) | Global Risks Survey, about 580 respondents, 37 risks | 10 years |
| 2012-2020 | likelihood, impact (top 5) | Global Risks Perception Survey, mean scores 1-5; risk set 50 (2012) to 30 (2020); definitions change most years | 10 years |
| 2014 | plus highest-concern (top 10) | pick-five-of-31, points 5 to 1 | 10 years |
| 2021 | likelihood, impact (top 7); horizon 0-2 / 3-5 / 5-10 years (top 10, % of respondents) | GRPS 2020, 35 risks, 1-5 scale; several risks merged | 10 years; bands |
| 2022 | severity 10-year (top 10); horizon 0-2 / 2-5 / 5-10 years (top 10, %) | GRPS 2021-2022, 37 risks; rank nine risks 1-9, points summed. No likelihood or impact ranking | 10 years; bands |
| 2023-2026 | 2-year, 10-year (top 10 each, by severity) | GRPS, severity on 1-7 Likert scale; labels rewritten in 2023 as long phrases, list revised each year (32-34 risks) | 2 and 10 years |
| 2024-2026 | plus current-year | 2024: pick up to five; 2025-2026: pick one. Shares not comparable between 2024 and 2025 | coming year |

## Sources used

- Each edition's own PDF on www3.weforum.org (all 2006-2026 downloaded and read, except 2015-2019, whose URLs were confirmed but not read).
- GRR 2014 Table 1.3 "The Evolving Global Risks Landscape (2007-2014)": labels and categories for 2007-2014.
- GRR 2020 Figure I "The Evolving Risks Landscape, 2007-2020": all 2007-2020 top 5; labels for 2015-2020.
- GRR 2021 Figure IV "Evolving Risks Landscape": cross-check for 2012-2020 (identical to GRR 2020 Figure I) and the 2021 top 7.

## Open problems and source oddities

1. **Retrospective figures relabel old risks.** GRR 2020 Figure I shortens and sometimes changes labels from earlier editions: "Blow up in asset prices" for "Asset price collapse" (2007-2008), "Deglobalization" for "Retrenchment from globalization", "Climate action failure" for "Climate change" (2014) and for "Failure of climate change adaptation" (2013 impact #5, a different concept). Files for 2007-2014 keep the GRR 2014 label as `label` and the GRR 2020 label as `label_grr2020_figure`. Treat these as `Revision` candidates, not noise.
2. **Categories disagree between retrospective figures.** Income disparity: societal in GRR 2014 for 2012-2013, economic in GRR 2020. Water supply crises: GRR 2014 footnote says reclassified environmental in 2014; GRR 2020 footnote says societal in 2015; the colours in each figure do not fully match their own footnotes. Retrenchment from globalization (emerging), 2009: geopolitical in GRR 2014, economic in GRR 2020.
3. **2007-2011 labels not checked against each edition's own report.** 2012 and 2013 were checked (same ranks and labels, scores added). 2007-2011 labels come from GRR 2014 Table 1.3.
4. **2015-2019 labels come from the GRR 2020 figure only.** Short labels; each edition's own wording not checked.
5. **2021 prints top 7**, not top 5 or 10, in its evolving-landscape figure.
6. **Ties.** 2012 likelihood #1 and #2 tie at 4.03; 2021, 2022 and 2025-2026 horizon or current-year bars have ties. Ties are ranked in printed order.
7. **Published dates are month-level** (January of each year). Exact launch days not verified.
8. **2026 PDF** has a modification date of 2026-02-03, after launch. A post-launch revision is possible and not checked.
9. Category is read from figure colour keys (the text layer has no category). 2014 "highest-concern" entries have no category.
