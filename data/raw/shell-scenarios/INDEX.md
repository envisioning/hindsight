# Shell scenarios: named scenario sets and their published pathways

Facts only: edition, scenario label, short premise quote, horizon, and scenario values for target years up to 2025. `claim_type: scenario`; graded on coverage only (D6). Nothing here is graded.

## Verify-first answers (checked 2026-10-01)

- **Index page.** Shell's own archive page "What are the previous Shell Scenarios?" (https://www.shell.com/news-and-insights/scenarios/what-are-the-previous-shell-scenarios.html, read via its `.model.json`) serves a PDF for every public global scenario set from 1993 to 2021, plus pages for 2023 and 2025. The current page (https://www.shell.com/news-and-insights/scenarios/the-2026-energy-security-scenarios.html) serves 2026. Shell lists its scenario work as starting in the early 1970s.
- **First public edition.** *Global Scenarios 1992-2020* (Shell lists it as 1993): New Frontiers and Barricades. It is the oldest set Shell serves. The 1970s and 1980s global scenarios were internal; none is served by Shell. A 1995 energy study, *The evolution of the world's energy system 1860-2060* (Sustained Growth, Dematerialisation), exists only as a bibliographic record (OSTI ETDEWEB 230531); its text is not public. Recorded as missing.
- **Editions up to 2026** (edition id = publication year as Shell lists it):
  - 1993 Global Scenarios 1992-2020 (New Frontiers, Barricades), horizon 2020. Image-only PDF; text from OCR.
  - 1996 Global Scenarios 1995-2020 (Just Do It!, Da Wo), horizon 2020.
  - 1999 Global Scenarios 1998-2020 (The New Game, People Power), horizon 2020.
  - 2001 Energy Needs, Choices and Possibilities: Scenarios to 2050 (Dynamics as Usual, The Spirit of the Coming Age), horizon 2050.
  - 2002 People and Connections: Global Scenarios to 2020 (Business Class, Prism), horizon 2020. Shell's 2005 report calls them "the 2001 Global Scenarios"; the public summary is dated February 2002.
  - 2005 Global Scenarios to 2025 (Low Trust Globalisation, Open Doors, Flags), horizon 2025. Only the 18-page public summary is free; the full book was sold.
  - 2007 Signposts (February 2007), a supplement that restates the 2005 scenarios. No new scenarios.
  - 2008 Shell Energy Scenarios to 2050 (Scramble, Blueprints), horizon 2050. *An Explorer's Guide* (2008) is a method guide with no scenarios; not an edition.
  - 2011 Signals and Signposts (February 2011), supplement to 2008. Names the internal Recession and Recovery outlooks and prints a primary energy table 2000-2030 "under current and expected policies".
  - 2013 New Lens Scenarios (Mountains, Oceans), March 2013, horizon 2060 (tables) / 2100. Prints summary quantification tables for 2020.
  - 2016 A Better Life with a Healthy Planet: Pathways to Net-Zero Emissions (May 2016), a New Lens supplement; one normative pathway, no new named scenario.
  - 2018 Sky: Meeting the Goals of the Paris Agreement (26 March 2018), horizon 2070/2100. Not on Shell's archive page; PDF from the Wayback Machine.
  - 2020 Rethinking the 2020s (September 2020), a scenario sketch (Waves / wealth first, Islands / security first, Sky / health first), horizon 2030. Text from the archived interactive app.
  - 2021 The Energy Transformation Scenarios (Waves, Islands, Sky 1.5), horizon 2100, with an underlying data workbook (xlsx).
  - 2023 The Energy Security Scenarios (Archipelagos, Sky 2050), 21 March 2023, horizon 2100, with an underlying data workbook (xlsb, May 2023 update).
  - 2025 The 2025 Energy Security Scenarios: Energy and artificial intelligence (Surge, Archipelagos, Horizon), horizon 2100, with a data workbook (xlsb).
  - 2026 The 2026 Energy Security Scenarios: Challenges to the transition (Archipelagos, Surge, Horizon), January 2026, horizon 2100, with a data workbook (xlsx).
  - No global set in 2024 (Shell lists none between 2023 and 2025). Regional "Scenarios where you live" sketches are out of scope.
- **What is not public.** Pre-1992 sets; the 1995 energy-system study; the full 2005 book; data tables for 1993-2018 editions (values exist in charts only, which NOTICE.md excludes, except the 2011 and 2013 printed tables).
- **Years that have passed.** Only values with target year <= 2025 are captured as `pathway` rows. In the data workbooks, years where every scenario has the same value are history or a shared base and are not captured: 2021 workbook diverges from 2020, 2023 from 2023, 2025 from 2025, 2026 only after 2025 (so the 2026 edition has no pathway rows).

## Subjects and label changes (Revisions)

- **Sky family.** Sky (2018, net zero 2070) -> Sky 1.5 (2021) -> Sky 2050 (2023, net zero 2050) -> Horizon (2025, 2026; Shell: "has been renamed Horizon"). Record each step as a `Revision` (renamed); the targets also change (2070 -> 2050). Rethinking the 2020s (2020) says its health-first decade "begins a pathway most like the Shell Sky scenario".
- **Waves / Islands.** First named in Rethinking the 2020s (2020) as decade pathways, then full scenarios in 2021. Same labels, horizon 2030 -> 2100: `Revision` (extended).
- **Archipelagos.** 2023, 2025, 2026. Shell says the 2026 version was revised (2.5°C in 2100 vs 2.2°C before): `Revision` (changed), not noise.
- **Surge.** New in 2025, kept in 2026.
- **Low Trust Globalisation / Open Doors / Flags.** 2005, restated unchanged in 2007 Signposts (same claims, second publication).
- **Mountains / Oceans** (2013) are referenced, not restated, in the 2016 supplement and the 2018 Sky booklet.
- **Metric definitions differ across workbooks.** 2021 "CO2 - energy" (GHG table) vs 2023/2025 "World net energy-related CO2 emissions" (after CCS) vs 2013 "Net CO2 emissions by point of emission" (rounded). 2021 "Solar" is all solar; 2023/2025 rows are "Solar - photovoltaic" only. Primary energy is Shell's own accounting (not IEA or EI conventions); compare with realised values only after converting conventions.

## What could not be read and why

- **Pre-1992 global scenarios** (1970s-1980s): internal; Shell serves none.
- **1995 energy-system study** (Sustained Growth, Dematerialisation, to 2060): only a conference/bibliographic record (OSTI ETDEWEB); no public text. Missing.
- **2005 full report**: sold as a book; only the executive summary is free (edition marked partial).
- **Chart values** in 1993-2018 editions (energy mix, GDP, oil demand charts): excluded by NOTICE.md. Only numbers printed in text or tables are captured.
- **Rethinking the 2020s (2020)**: interactive app; charts are "indicative only" and carry no printed numbers for years <= 2025.
- **Sky (2018) data tables**: Shell published Sky data separately at the time; not located on the current site or tried on the Wayback Machine. Text values only.
- **2008 Scramble/Blueprints quantities**: charts only in the PDF; no data table found.
- The 2023 and 2025 page links end in `.xls`, but the server returns 404 for that name; the same paths with `.xlsb` work (found via the Wayback CDX index).

## Open problems

- **OCR (1993).** The 1993 PDF is scanned images. Quotes were reassembled from OCR of a two-column layout; pathway rows are `medium`. Recheck against the PDF image before publishing quotes.
- **Reassembled sentences.** 1996 energy-intensity sentence and 2005 "40% higher" bullet are interleaved with sidebars in the text layer; `medium`.
- **Qualitative pathway rows.** Several pathway rows have no `value` (milestones such as "gas overtakes coal by 2010", "EV cost parity by 2025"). They are dated scenario statements; graders will need a measure per row.
- **2021 Sky 1.5 peak coal 2014.** The milestone table puts Sky 1.5's coal peak in 2014, i.e. in history (Waves 2031, Islands 2028). It reads as "coal never again exceeds 2014" in that scenario; the workbook's Sky 1.5 coal values for 2023-2025 (158.6-160.7 EJ) imply 2014 was higher in Shell's history series. Not verified against the workbook's 2014 history (the workbook has 2015 and 2019 history columns only).
- **2021 CO2 2020.** "CO2 - energy" for 2020 is identical in all three 2021 scenarios (32.528 Gt), while primary energy differs. Treat 2020 CO2 as a shared estimate, not a scenario difference.
- **2021 and 2025 publication months** not confirmed (PDF metadata are later re-saves).
- **Upper-bound values.** "just under 4%", "nearly a tenth", "over 300 million", "below $500", "fewer than 50" are stored as the stated number with the qualifier in `unit`.
- **1996 booklet dates.** The public booklet's footers are dated 1999 while Shell lists the set as 1996; the scenarios cover 1995-2020. Edition id follows Shell's listing.
- **2011 table** is labelled "projections under current and expected policies", not a named scenario; it is the closest Shell came to a base case and should be graded as a scenario value, not a forecast (Shell's disclaimer).
- **Explorer's Guide (2008)** is listed by Shell with the scenarios but is a method guide; not captured as an edition.
