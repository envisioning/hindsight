## Editions 2005 to 2017: digitized positions

Every Gartner Hype Cycle for Emerging Technologies from 2005 to 2017, digitized from the published charts.

### Files

`2005.json` to `2017.json`. 492 entries across 13 editions, 211 technologies.

Each entry:

- `label`: the technology as printed on the chart, including any typo in the source sheet.
- `x_time`: position along the curve, 0 at the left edge of the chart to 100 at the right edge.
- `y_expectations`: height on the curve, 0 at the bottom to 100 at the top.
- `time_to_plateau_code`: Gartner's marker, coded 1 (less than 2 years), 2 (2 to 5), 3 (5 to 10), 4 (more than 10), 5 (obsolete before plateau).
- `band`: the same marker as text.
- `technology_index`: a grouping of the same technology across editions. Slight rewordings are kept apart on purpose, because Gartner may reword to change meaning.
- `label_in_combined`: present when the combined source table spells the label differently from the per-year workbook. The per-year spelling is kept as `label`.

Phase is not recorded. It depends on where each year's chart puts its phase boundaries, which vary by edition. Derive it per edition before grading.

### Source files not included

The Gartner chart images used for digitization are Gartner's copyright and are not in this repository (NOTICE.md). Only the extracted positions and codes are here.

### Checks

- 2016: all 34 labels and all 26 bands that Hindsight read independently from Gartner's press-release chart match this data exactly. This data fills the 8 bands Hindsight could not read.
- 2017: all 32 entries match an independent text transcription of the chart (Sohu); all 28 directly compared bands match.
